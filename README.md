# Linear A: reproducible exploratory research

This repository continues a staged investigation of Linear A sign patterns, accounting contexts, and possible morphology. It is not a decipherment and does not assign unverified translations.

## Current work

Phase 4 moves from a historical word index to the machine-readable inscription corpus maintained by Robert Hogan at `mwenge/lineara.xyz`. The analysis will pin an immutable upstream commit, preserve damage information from the original-sign `words` array, exclude the speculative English `translatedWords` field, and report both positive comparisons and counterexamples.

Previously prioritized comparisons include `TA-NA-TE / A-TA-NA-TE` on ZA 10, `PA-RA-NE / A-PA-RA-NE` on HT 115 / HT 96, and `A-RA-TU / A-RA-TU-ME` on ZA 7 / HT Wc 3024. These are candidates, not established grammatical paradigms or claims of new epigraphic discoveries.

Code, tests, source provenance, and a report will be added as the investigation proceeds. Do not treat intermediate results as a validated language identification.

## Source and method cautions

The digital corpus derives from published editions and does not replace their photographs, drawings, or apparatus. Absence of a machine-readable damage flag is not proof that an inscription is complete or certain. Distinct faces of one object are not independent objects. Exact sign-string containment does not itself identify an affix.
