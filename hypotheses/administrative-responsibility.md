# H2: initial A and administrative responsibility

[All hypotheses](README.md) | [Evidence register](../docs/evidence.md) | [Review](../docs/review.md)

**Status: low-confidence conjecture; the responsibility meaning is not currently preferred.** The paired forms remain research targets. Neither their grammatical relationship nor its meaning has been independently identified. Literature priority is unestablished.

## Original proposed readings

```text
HT 96b: A-PA-RA-NE       "Under Parane's responsibility."
ZA 10a: TA-NA-TE 2 ... A-TA-NA-TE 1
                         "Tanate: two. ... On Tanate's account: one."
```

These are two applications of one unvalidated relationship, not independent translations. Parane and Tanate are convenient renderings of conventional sign labels; the labels' identities and pronunciations are not established. The ellipsis on ZA 10 stands for intervening entries, not missing words from a continuous sentence. "Account" is part of the conjectural English explanation, not a decoded noun.

## Why the comparisons remain useful

PA-RA-NE occurs as an entry on HT 115, while A-PA-RA-NE occurs in heading material on HT 96. Both objects are assigned to HT Scribe 8. The two Tanate forms occur on the same tablet, ZA 10. Those comparisons reduce some regional or scribal explanations, but do not prove that each pair refers to the same entity.[1,2]

HT 115 is a palimpsest. The numeral beside PA-RA-NE on its a face is doubtful; its b face supplies another entry with preceding text broken. On HT 96b, QA-*118-RA-RE and additional notation remain uninterpreted. The listed commodities are conventionally grain 40+J+E, OLE+U 4, and FIC 2+K, with J doubtful.[1]

## Pair-count evidence is model-dependent

The six candidate initial-A pairs do not independently establish a morpheme or an administrative meaning. The default analysis now conditions on the numbers of longer types, attested remainders, and candidate-edge types within each word length. The original fitted-rate model is retained separately.[3]

| Scope | Pairs | Conditional expected | Conditional p | Conditional Holm | Fitted-rate p | Fitted-rate Holm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All 599 eligible types | 6 | 3.75 | 0.15367 | 1.00000 | 0.12162 | 1.00000 |
| 437 tablet types | 6 | 2.00 | 0.00765 | 0.45881 | 0.00105 | 0.06271 |

The correction covers 66 initial signs across all types and 60 within tablets. Neither model yields a corrected result below 0.05. Non-rejection is not proof of coincidence, and even a robust excess would not select responsibility over possession, origin, another grammatical relationship, or distinct names. The conditional model still assumes within-length exchangeability and does not capture all linguistic dependencies.

The candidate set includes KI-RO/A-KI-RO and SA-RA2/A-SA-RA2. The shorter forms have accounting uses, so the six pairs cannot all be assumed to be personal names. Restricting the conjecture to selected name-like pairs does not itself demonstrate which pairs belong to that class.

## Scope and alternatives

This is not a rule stripping A from every A-initial expression. A prefix, a written-together particle, another grammatical relationship, and distinct lexical forms remain alternatives. Both KA-RU and A-KA-RU have heading uses, so A cannot simply be equated with heading position. Quantity does not determine grammatical number: a named entity can receive multiple units.

ZA 10 also has A-KU-MI-NA and A-DU-KU-MI-NA. Their DU contrast is worth preserving as an observation, but it does not independently validate segmentation or a meaning for A-DU.[2]

**Retain the same-scribe and same-object comparisons; do not prefer "responsibility" as their meaning.** A consistent difference in administrative role must be established without deriving that role from the proposed translation. Independently identified distinct referents would weaken the same-entity interpretation. None of the existing applications was a held-out semantic prediction.
