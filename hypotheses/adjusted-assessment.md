# H1: DA-DU-MA-TA as an adjusted assessment

[All hypotheses](README.md) | [Evidence register](../docs/evidence.md) | [Review](../docs/review.md)

**Status: low-confidence conjecture; not a preferred translation.** The proposed meaning is unvalidated and has only one exact occurrence in the pinned corpus. The closest comparison supplies a plausible alternative, not a direct falsification. First-in-literature priority is unestablished.

## Original proposal

For HT 95a:

> **Reassessed grain allotment:** DA-ME 10; MI-NU-TE 10; SA-RU 20; KU-NI-SU 10; DI-DE-RU 10; QE-RA2-U 7.

This assigns an accounting-state meaning to `DA-DU-MA-TA`. It does not establish whether the expression instead names a person, place, institution, commodity, or another category. The other labels remain untranslated; grain is the conventional reading of GRA. The English sentence is retained to document the conjecture, not offered as a recovered meaning.

## Evidence on HT 95

The headings are `DA-DU-MA-TA . GRA` on face a and `A-DU` on face b. Aligning the six corresponding readable entries by label gives:[1]

| Entry | Face b | Face a | a minus b |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| DA-ME | 10 | 10 | 0 |
| MI-NU-TE | 10 | 10 | 0 |
| SA-RU | 10 | 20 | +10 |
| KU-NI-SU | 10 | 10 | 0 |
| DI-DE-RU | 10 | 10 | 0 |
| QE-RA2-U | 10 | 7 | -3 |
| Calculated subtotal of these entries | 60 | 67 | +7 |

These subtotals are not inscribed totals. A damaged interval on b prevents certifying its complete total, some label readings on a are doubtful, and GRA is explicit only on a. Comparison assumes matching commodities and units. Face letters are not dates. The general assessment interpretation is published scholarship, attributed by Younger to Schoep 2002, p. 106.[1]

## The closest comparison: HT 86

Younger's HT 95 commentary explicitly points to HT 86. That comparison should have been examined before preferring a revision interpretation. HT 86a shares **five exact labels**, not six:[2]

```text
A-KA-RU
  KU-NI-SU    grain-related notation    20
  SA-RU                                20
  DI-DE-RU                             20
  QA-RA2-WA                            10
-----------------------------------------
A-DU
  DA-ME       different grain notation 20
  MI-NU-TE                             20
```

`QA-RA2-WA` is not HT 95's `QE-RA2-U`. The first section's notation is normalized as GRA+K+L in the digital corpus and written GRA+K+L2 in the commentary; the second uses GRA+B. Different notation does not by itself identify a commodity, transaction, or accounting-state contrast. HT 86b preserves an erased partial repetition; the commentary describes the object as a double palimpsest.[2]

The comparison shows recurring entities or labels partitioned under different headings. It makes separate accounts, categories, or transaction sections concrete alternatives to reassessment. **It does not directly refute the proposed meaning of `DA-DU-MA-TA`: that expression does not occur on HT 86.** No validated prediction required every A-DU section to be a reference schedule for a whole tablet.

A-DU's census also needs care: ten occurrences across three sites, seven in first parsed rows, five in rows classed as headings by the heuristic, and seven machine-unflagged occurrences. These overlapping counts do not establish ten secure heading uses or a common-noun meaning.[3]

## Assessment and alternatives

The +10 and -3 differences are consistent with adjustment, but they do not identify revision as their cause. A uniform conversion or a reductions-only rule cannot account for them under the same-unit assumption. Eliminating those narrow alternatives does not select reassessment over separate accounts, assessment versus receipts, different commodities or periods, or another order of revision.

**Retain the account comparison; do not privilege the English gloss.** Another independently interpretable occurrence of DA-DU-MA-TA, or a reference/revision pair whose states are known without using this hypothesis, would be needed to support the proposed meaning. The current numerical agreement is not a semantic validation.
